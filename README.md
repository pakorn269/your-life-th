# Your Life (ชีวิตของคุณ)

A visualization of your life, divided into years, months, and weeks. Now fully supports Thai language, Buddhist Era (พ.ศ.) year input, and features a modern Bootstrap 5 UI.

## Demos
- **[ชีวิตของคุณในแต่ละปี (Your life in Years)](https://pakorn269.github.io/your-life-th/years.html)**
- **[ชีวิตของคุณในแต่ละเดือน (Your life in Months)](https://pakorn269.github.io/your-life-th/months.html)**
- **[ชีวิตของคุณในแต่ละสัปดาห์ (Your life in Weeks)](https://pakorn269.github.io/your-life-th/weeks.html)**

## Features
- Thai language interface and fonts
- Buddhist Era (พ.ศ.) year input (auto-converted for calculation)
- Responsive, modern UI with Bootstrap 5
- Visualizes your life progress in years, months, and weeks
